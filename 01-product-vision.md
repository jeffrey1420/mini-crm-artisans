# Mini-CRM Product Vision

## The Problem We Solve

French artisans—plumbers, electricians, carpenters, construction workers—run their businesses with **paper notebooks, WhatsApp, and memory**. They lose track of customers, miss follow-ups, forget to send invoices, and can't remember when they last contacted a lead.

**The core pain:**
> "Je connais tous mes clients, mais je ne sais plus quand je dois les rappeler." (I know all my clients, but I don't remember when I should call them back.)

## For Whom

- **Artisans aged 30-55** who run their own business (1-10 employees)
- **Micro-entreprises** and **artisans Tayloristes** in construction, plumbing, electrical, carpentry
- **380,000+ businesses** in France fitting this profile
- Tech-averse but smartphone-literate; WhatsApp-native

## What We Build

A **dead-simple CRM** that feels like a digital notebook, not enterprise software:

1. **Contacts** — Save客户 info, addresses (French format), photos of work
2. **Pipeline** — Where is each project? Devis → Accepté → En cours → Terminé
3. **Reminders** — "Rappeler M. Dupont dans 3 jours"
4. **Job Tracking** — What work was done, what materials used

## How It Feels

- **Mobile-first** — artisans are on-site, not at a desk
- **One-handed operation** — big buttons, simple taps
- **French language** — everything in French, culturally familiar
- **Forgiving** — works offline, syncs when connected
- **No training needed** — if they can use WhatsApp, they can use this

## The Vision in One Sentence

> **"Le carnet de commandes numérique qui tient dans la poche"** — A digital order book that fits in your pocket.

## Success Metrics

- Customer retention rate (do they keep using it after trial?)
- Daily active usage (do they open it every day?)
- Revenue per user (can we expand to invoicing later?)
- NPS score (would they recommend it?)
