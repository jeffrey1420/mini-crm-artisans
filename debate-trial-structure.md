# Debate 36: D6 — Free Tier IS the Trial (Resolving the Conflict)

**Challenge:** TODO.md says D6 = "30 days (kept from previous)" but debate-log.md (Debate 26, 11:11) resolved D6 = 14 days. This is a data error. But the error reveals a deeper structural problem.

### Product Strategist — Free Tier + 14-Day Trial Creates Cognitive Dissonance

**Core argument:** The 14-day trial was designed for a paid-only product (no Free tier). Now that D33 resolved Free + €29 two-tier, adding a 14-day trial on top creates redundant commitment friction and cognitive dissonance. The Free tier IS the trial — it removes time pressure while still enabling full product experience (up to limits).

**Why Free + 14-Day Trial is a broken model:**
1. **Two commitment devices conflict.** Free tier says "try without commitment, no time pressure." 14-day countdown says "decide now or lose access." These are contradictory signals for a simplicity-first product. The artisan is simultaneously told "take your time" and "time is running out."
2. **The Free tier already solves the trial problem.** Up to 10 clients and 5 active devis is enough for a solo artisan to experience core value. If they outgrow it in week 1, they convert. If they don't outgrow it in 3 months, they weren't a serious buyer anyway.
3. **14-day countdown adds anxiety, not urgency.** For a 45-55 year old who signed up casually, a countdown email on day 11 ("3 days left!") creates anxiety — "I haven't had time to use this, I should cancel before I'm charged." The trial ends with guilt, not confidence. The Free tier never creates this anxiety.
4. **The conversion moment is hitting the limit, not a countdown.** Free → Paid conversion happens when the artisan says "I need more than 10 clients" — a natural, needs-driven decision. Countdown → Paid conversion happens under pressure — leading to churn at month 2 when the guilt wears off.

**The correct model:**
- Free tier = acquisition (no friction, no time pressure, word-of-mouth friendly)
- Paid conversion = hitting the Free limit OR manual upgrade
- No 14-day countdown — the "trial" is using Free until you need more

**VERDICT:** D6 should be updated: **No 14-day countdown trial. Free tier IS the trial.** When an artisan hits 10 clients or 5 active devis, that's the upgrade conversation. Remove the day-countdown email drip. Keep the day-7 check-in ("How's it going? Need anything?") as a human touch, not a countdown.

- OLD: `Trial = 14 days (updated from 30). No credit card at signup. Email drip: day 7, 3, 1.`
- NEW: `No time-limited trial. Free tier IS the trial (10 clients, 5 active devis). Conversion happens when artisan hits Free limit. No countdown emails. Day-7 human check-in only.`
