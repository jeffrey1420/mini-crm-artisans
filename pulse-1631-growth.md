## Debate 72: Expert-Comptable Referrals Should Not Be Phase 2

**Challenge:** D55 resolved expert-comptable outreach is Phase 2 (6-18 month build). This assumption was never debated — it was noted and deferred.

### Core Argument

**The expert-comptable is the economic buyer for the admin handler persona — and the admin handler is the operational user who converts.**

D55 correctly identified two personas: Marc (economic buyer, primary) and the admin handler (operational user, secondary). But the expert-comptable referral pathway was assigned to Phase 2 based on the assumption that building that relationship takes 6-18 months. That's cold outreach timeline. Louis already has an expert-comptable.

**Louis has an existing expert-comptable. That's Phase 1 access.**

Every French business owner has an accountant. Louis is no exception. One conversation with Louis's own accountant = immediate warm introduction to a professional network that already trusts expert-comptable software recommendations. The "6-18 month build" applies to cold outreach. This is not cold.

**The expert-comptable is not just a referral source — they recommend software to their clients routinely.**

Expert-comptables already have processes for recommending tools: Sage, Pennylane, Indy, Cegid. They evaluate software, negotiate group rates, sometimes mandate usage for their client base. This is a known professional service they provide, not a personal favor being asked. The relationship is already professional.

**Every day without expert-comptable outreach is a day the admin handler persona is unaddressed.**

Digital channels (WhatsApp groups, Facebook) reach Marc. But the admin handler — the person who would actually use the software daily — doesn't discover tools through WhatsApp artisan groups. She discovers them through her accountant, through peer networks of other admin handlers, through GetApp/Capterra. If we're not reaching her in Phase 1, we're not reaching our actual conversion target.

### Proposed Alternative

Move expert-comptable outreach from Phase 2 to Phase 1, running parallel to digital channel development.

**Immediate action (this week):** Louis asks his own accountant: (1) do you recommend software to clients? (2) would you be open to a demo? (3) can you introduce us to 2-3 other accountants?

**Phase 1 Expert-Comptable Protocol:**
- Warm intro via existing accountant → 2-3 additional accountants in month 1
- Demo the product: focus on admin handler value (time saved on devis/facture management, not Marc's pain)
- Leave one-pager: key stats, pricing, how to refer clients
- Target: 3 expert-comptables referencing 50+ sole trader clients by Month 2

### Verdict on D55

**REOPENED** — Expert-comptable outreach should be Phase 1, not Phase 2. The 6-18 month timeline is for cold outreach. Louis's existing accountant is a warm entry point. U12 (expert-comptable GTM playbook) should be actioned immediately, not deferred.
