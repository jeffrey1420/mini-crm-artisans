# Social Media & Content Roadmap — Mini-CRM

## Objectives

1. **Build brand awareness** among French artisans (plumbers, electricians, carpenters) within the first 6 months
2. **Establish thought leadership** as the go-to CRM/resource for artisan business management
3. **Generate organic lead flow** through educational content (no paid ads initially)
4. **Build community** of French artisans sharing best practices around business management
5. **Drive product adoption** by demonstrating real-world use cases specific to each trade
6. **Create content once, repurpose many times** across all platforms to maximize zero-budget output

## Subdomains

- **Content Creation**: Video, graphics, copy for all platforms
- **Community Management**: Engagement, comments, DMs, Facebook Groups
- **Analytics & Optimization**: Performance tracking, A/B testing, reporting
- **Influencer/Ambassador Relations**: Artisan partner identification and collaboration
- **Trend Monitoring**: Seasonal topics, tax changes, industry news
- **Crisis & Policy**: Guidelines, escalation procedures, compliance

## Milestones

| Month | Milestone |
|-------|-----------|
| Month 1 | Profile setup across all platforms; first 30 posts published; hashtag research complete |
| Month 2 | 60 posts published; first engagement campaigns; ambassador outreach begins |
| Month 3 | 90 posts; first UGC featured; community guidelines finalized; analytics dashboard live |
| Month 4 | Cross-platform repurposing workflow established; 2 artisan ambassadors onboarded |
| Month 5 | 120+ posts; social listening active; trending calendar populated |
| Month 6 | Full analytics reporting; crisis management doc live; 5+ ambassadors active |
