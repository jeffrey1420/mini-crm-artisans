---

ARGUMENT 1: THE ICP CLAIM IS UNSUPPORTED AND LIKELY FALSE

Grinto is a B2B SaaS company. Louis works there. Grinto's clients are businesses that buy software tools for operations.

Mini-CRM's ICP is: French artisans — plumbers, electricians, small contractors — who manage client relationships, quotes, and follow-ups manually.

These are not the same customer.

An expert-comptable serves artisans directly. An expert-comptable's client base IS the ICP. Grinto's client base is businesses that have budget for B2B SaaS. These overlap only accidentally.

If Grinto sells project management software, ERP tools, or business operations platforms, their clients are SMBs — some of which may be artisans, but many of which will be consultancies, agencies, small retailers, and other businesses that are nothing like a plumber with 3 employees managing his schedule on WhatsApp.

GS-0517 says "many of them are artisans or serve artisans directly" — this is a guess dressed up as a fact. The 05:17 paper provides zero evidence that Grinto's actual client base skews toward tradespeople.

The expert-comptable has 80-150 artisan clients. Louis cannot tell you how many of Grinto's clients are artisans because Louis doesn't know — and neither does GS-0517.

ARGUMENT 2: THE GRINTO TIMELINE ARGUMENT COLLAPSES IF THE ICP IS WRONG

GS-0517 claims Grinto client intros take 1-2 weeks vs 3-6 weeks for expert-comptable.

But if Grinto's clients are the wrong ICP, Louis spends 2 weeks getting warm intros to people who don't need Mini-CRM, and then has to start over anyway. The "fast" path becomes the slowest path because it leads nowhere.

Expert-comptable intros are slower but are at least pointed at the right destination.

The timeline comparison only matters if both channels are pointing at the same ICP. They are not.

GS-0517 spent paragraphs arguing against slow multi-hop channels while ignoring the fastest possible 1-hop question.
