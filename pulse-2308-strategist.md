# Pulse 2308 — Product Strategist

## Debate: B — Guided Creation Flow (D93)

**Counter-Assumption:** That mandatory Guided Creation Flow increases retention and identifies high-quality leads.

---

## Core Argument

The Guided Creation Flow assumes hand-holding converts. It doesn't — it converts *for* hand-holders.

Marc, our 45-55yo French BTP artisan, bought this product to escape a process. He's currently using WhatsApp voice notes, paper notebooks, and Excel spreadsheets held together by anxiety. He doesn't want to be onboarded. He wants to open the app, add a client, and send a devis. The Guided Creation Flow — a bookable 10-15 minute slot with someone "helping" him set up — is a friction point disguised as value-add.

Here's the selection bias: who books the onboarding call? Not the autonomous professional who's been running his business for 20 years. He sees "15 minute call" and thinks "I don't have time for this bullshit." He leaves. The person who books? The newly self-employed, the数字化新手, the one who's uncertain — the artisan who will also need the most support every single time something goes wrong. You've optimized for acquiring your highest-cost, lowest-LTV user.

Meanwhile, the autonomous pro — your ideal customer — interprets "we'll walk you through it" as "this product assumes I can't figure it out." That's a trust signal in the wrong direction. He's been buying tools his whole career. He reads the features, he tries it, he decides. The Guided Creation Flow tells him he's not the target.

And here's the kicker: Sprint 0 is building infrastructure for a call that 70% of your free-to-paid conversions will skip entirely. You're investing engineering time in a friction point that your best customers navigate around, and your worst customers embrace instead.

The real question isn't "how do we onboard Marc?" It's "why would Marc need onboarding for something this simple?"

## Verdict

Kill the mandatory Guided Creation Flow. Replace it with: (1) a 90-second in-app setup wizard that pre-populates with sensible defaults, and (2) a genuinely helpful "comment ça marche" video accessible on demand. Let Marc choose his own adventure. If he needs help, offer it. If he doesn't, don't insult him by requiring it. Day-7 retention improves when you remove barriers — not when you add guided lanes.
