# User Personas — French Artisans

## Persona 1: Marc, 42 — Plombier Indépendant

### Demographics
- **Age:** 42
- **Métier:** Plombier-chauffagiste (independent plumber/heating specialist)
- **Entreprise:** Auto-entrepreneur, 1 personne
- **Zone:** Rural Normandy (village of ~3,000 people)
- **Revenue估计:** €35,000-45,000/year
- **Tech level:** Can use WhatsApp, prend des photos, pas d'ordinateur

### A Day in His Life
- 6:30 — Réveil, vérifie les messages WhatsApp des clients
- 7:00 — Première intervention (panne chauffage)
- 10:30 — Devis chez un nouveau client
- 12:30 — Pause déjeuner (souvent dans le camion)
- 14:00 — Deuxième intervention
- 17:00 — Retour au bureau (la table de la cuisine), lance l'ordi pour les factures
- 20:30 — Cuisine, famille, **il oublie de rappeler les clients**

### Pain Points
> "J'ai un vieux carnet où j'écris mes rendez-vous. Des fois je ne retrouve plus le numéro de quelqu'un. Quand un client appelle pour un dépannage, je ne sais plus ce qu'on s'est dit la dernière fois."

- Uses **paper notebook** for appointments (illegible after 2 weeks)
- WhatsApp messages get buried
- No system for follow-ups
- Forgets to send devis
- Loses track of supplier references

### What He Wants
- Something he can use **on his phone while in the truck**
- **Pas compliqués** — if it takes 5 minutes to learn, he'll abandon it
- Reminders that actually **ring and interrupt** (not just a notification)
- His **existing client data** accessible (phone numbers, addresses)

### Willingness to Pay
- Currently pays nothing for any business software
- Would consider **€29/month** if it saves him 1 hour/week
- Would NOT pay €79/month (too expensive for solo)

## Persona 2: Sophie, 38 — Électricienne avec 2 Employés

### Demographics
- **Age:** 38
- **Métier:** Électricienne artisanale (electrical contractor)
- **Entreprise:** SARL, 3 personnes (elle + 2 electriciens)
- **Zone:** Peri-urban Rhône-Alpes (suburb of Lyon)
- **Revenue估计:** €120,000-180,000/year
- **Tech level:** Comfortable with smartphones, uses Dropbox for photos

### A Day in Her Life
- 7:00 — Revue des chantiers du jour avec son équipe
- 8:00 — Chantier residential (rénovation appartement)
- 12:00 — Déjeuner avec client potentiel (prospection)
- 14:00 — Deuxième chantier
- 17:00 — Bureau (elle a un vrai bureau!) — facturation, devis, appels
- 19:00 — Home, dinner, but **she's still thinking about tomorrow's follow-ups**

### Pain Points
> "On a essayé un tableur Excel pour suivre les devis. C'est moi qui le mets à jour, les gars ne comprennent rien à Excel. J'ai besoin de quelque chose qu'on puisse utiliser tous."

- Excel is **too complex for her employees** to use
- No shared view of pipeline across the team
- **Loses quotes** (devis) — where are they in the process?
- WhatsApp groups are **chaos** — 47 unread messages
- Forgets to remind employees of appointments

### What She Wants
- **Shared pipeline** visible to her and her 2 employees
- **Simple status updates** — "Où en est le devis pour M. Martin?"
- **Automatic reminders** for follow-ups
- Integration with **Google Calendar** (she uses it)

### Willingness to Pay
- Currently paying €0 for CRM (Excel is free)
- Would pay **€49-59/month** for team-shared solution
- Needs **multi-user** (at least 3)
- Would consider €79/month if it replaces 2-3 other tools

## Persona 3: Jean-Pierre, 55 — Charpentier Traditionaliste

### Demographics
- **Age:** 55
- **Métier:** Charpentier (carpenter, traditional wooden framing)
- **Entreprise:** Artisan traditionnel, 6 employés (PME)
- **Zone:** Rural Bourgogne (small town, 8,000 people)
- **Revenue估计:** €350,000-500,000/year
- **Tech level:** **Résistant** — uses a Nokia (not smartphone!) for calls, PC at office for emails

### A Day in His Life
- 6:00 — Usine (atelier), prepare les commandes bois
- 7:00 — Chantier (construction bois massive)
- 12:00 — Repas avec les équipes
- 13:30 — Chantier continues
- 18:00 — Bureau — il a une **secrétaire** qui gère l'administratif
- She uses a computer; **he does not touch it**

### Pain Points
> "Moi, je connais mon métier. La gestion, c'est pas mon job. Ma femme (secrétaire) fait tout. Je veux juste savoir où on en est avec les clients sans avoir à demander."

- **Does not use smartphone** for business tasks
- Relies 100% on his **secrétaire** for admin
- She currently uses **paper + Excel**
- No visibility into pipeline unless he asks
- **Resistant to change** — "J'ai 55 ans, je ne vais pas apprendre un nouveau logiciel"

### What He Wants
- **Voice input** — "Ajoute un nouveau client" — too hard to type
- **Someone else manages it** (the secretary)
- **Weekly summary** on paper or email he can read
- **Nothing that requires him to change his behavior**

### Willingness to Pay
- Currently pays €150/month for an accountant
- Would pay **€79/month** if secretary recommends it
- Key question: **Can his secretary use it easily?**

## Key Insights

1. **Mobile-first is non-negotiable** — most artisans are on-site, not at a desk
2. **WhatsApp is the baseline** — if it's harder than WhatsApp, they'll quit
3. **Secretary/administrateur is often the real user** in larger shops
4. **Voice input could be huge** for older artisans like Jean-Pierre
5. **Simplicity > Features** — one-button actions beat dropdown menus
