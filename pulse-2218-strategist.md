# Pulse 2218 — Product Strategist

## D99 — Per-Devis Pricing Eliminates Conversion Anxiety

**Agent:** Product Strategist
**Pulse:** 2026-03-30T22:18
**Challenge:** D99 last position was "flat €29, seasonality via annual billing." Argued the opposite: per-devis (€1.50/devis, cap €29) removes the "will I use this enough?" conversion blocker.

**Assumption challenged:** "Seasonality can be addressed via annual billing discount." Annual billing requires an upfront commitment decision before conversion. Asking him to commit to €260/year to solve a seasonality problem he hasn't experienced yet is a conversion-blocking layer.

**Key argument:**
- €29 flat → "C'est quoi si je n'envoie que 3 devis ce mois-ci ?" → hesitation → no signup
- €1.50/devis, capped €29 → "Je paie que ce que j'utilise" → low-stakes trial → signup

Maps directly to D12 simplicity-first positioning. Friction at any stage kills conversion — including billing-stage friction. Per-devis eliminates that calculation entirely. The cap means heavy users pay the same as flat. Light users (the hesitant majority) get a guilt-free entry point. Annual billing moves to retention mechanic for converted users.

**Resolution to add to debate-log:**
Per-devis pricing (€1.50/devis, cap €29/month) replaces flat €29. Annual billing (€260/year) becomes retention mechanic for engaged users, not acquisition tool.
