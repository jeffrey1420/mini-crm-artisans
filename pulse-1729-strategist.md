# Pulse 2026-03-30T17:29 — Product Strategist

## Debate 77: "Membre fondateur" Creates Price Anxiety It Was Meant to Resolve

**Challenge:** D75 resolved that "Membre fondateur" framing (4 benefits: locked price + named in app + direct founder access + roadmap vote) creates relationship and urgency without training users to wait for discounts. This assumption is wrong.

### Core Argument

**The Structural Flaw:**

D75's founding member framing has a fundamental trust problem: it signals that the price is *temporary* even when the locked-price benefit is genuine. Think about how a French artisan processes "Membre fondateur":

- "Founding member" = early adopter benefit = *special price for pioneers*
- If there's a special price for pioneers → there must be a normal price that comes later
- Result: €29/month is perceived as the *promotional offer*, not the real price

This is cognitive dissonance the framing creates, not resolves.

### Assumptions Challenged

**Assumption 1 challenged:** "Creates relationship without training users to wait for discounts."

The opposite happens. "Membre fondateur" *is* a discount framing. It tells users: "This is what you pay *now*, as a favor to us. Later, it will cost more." That's a discount signal wearing relationship language.

**Assumption 2 challenged:** "First 50 slots creates natural scarcity."

Natural scarcity works when the product has demonstrated demand. At launch, you have *zero* traction. "50 founding spots" sounds arbitrary when nobody knows if the product works. It's a scarcity construct without social proof backing it.

### The Latecomer Problem

Marc signs up in Month 3. He sees "50 founding members" — he wasn't invited. Either:
- (a) He feels he missed something valuable → resentment
- (b) He waits for "cohort 2 founding membership" → indefinitely delayed conversion

You've trained him to wait for the next founding offer.

### The Alternative

**"Essai gratuit 14 jours, puis €29/mois. Prix définitif. Sans engagement."**

Translation: Free 14-day trial, then €29/month. Final price. No commitment.

This framing:
- Makes €29 the *normal, stated price* — not a promotional one
- Eliminates "what will I pay later?" anxiety
- Trial-first respects Marc's skepticism (he's right to be skeptical)
- "Sans engagement" reduces friction for a solo artisan with cash flow concerns

### Verdict

**D75's "Membre fondateur" framing creates price anxiety it was meant to resolve.** The locked-price benefit is good; the founding member wrapper is not. A clean "trial → €29/month, final price" positioning converts better because it removes the implied question: *"What will I pay after founding member pricing ends?"*

### New Action Items

1. **A/B test:** "Membre fondateur" (locked €29) vs "Essai gratuit → €29/mois, prix définitif" — run with first 20 signups
2. **Remove founding member language** from onboarding if D75 stands — don't let it leak into in-app messaging where late adopters will see it
3. **If scarcity is needed:** Use seats remaining (e.g., "17 places restantes ce mois") tied to *actual* capacity, not arbitrary cohort numbers
