# Go-to-Market Strategy — Mini-CRM for French Artisans

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 0: Soft Launch (Month 0-2)
**Goal:** 50 beta users, gather feedback, iterate

**Channel:** Personal network
- Post in French entrepreneur Facebook groups
- Post in "auto-entrepreneurs" subreddits (r/france)
- Ask friends who are artisans to try free

**Tactic:** Free 3-month trial for 50 beta users
- Get real feedback
- Build testimonials
- Fix critical bugs

---

## Acquisition Channels

### 1. Word of Mouth (Primary) — Weight: 40%

**Why:** Artisans trust each other more than ads. They talk at:
- Dépôts de matériaux (building suppliers: Point.P, BigMat, VM)
- Guild meetings (Chambre des Métars)
- Trade fairs (BATIMAT, ESCT)
- WhatsApp groups

**Tactics:**
- Ask beta users to refer 1 friend → €20 credit
- Attend local trade fairs (1-2 per year per region)
- Partner with building material suppliers

### 2. Content Marketing (SEO) — Weight: 25%

**Why:** "Comment gérer mes clients artisan" searches exist

**Content plan:**
- Blog posts in French:
  - "Comment suivre ses clients en tant qu'artisan?"
  - "Logiciel CRM pour plombier: le guide complet"
  - "Gérer ses devis et clients sans Excel"
- YouTube channel:
  - "Tutoriel Mini-CRM en 2 minutes"
  - "Comment un plombier a retrouvé tous ses clients"

**SEO keywords (French):**
- "CRM artisan" (low competition)
- "logiciel gestion client artisan"
- "suivi client artisan du bâtiment"
- "application CRM artisan"

### 3. Social Media — Weight: 20%

**Platforms:**
- **Instagram** — visual, artisan audience present
  - Before/after renovation photos
  - Tips: "3 tricks to never miss a client call"
  - Reels: quick tutorials

- **TikTok** — underrated for B2B
  - "Day in the life of a French plumber"
  - Humorous takes on "managing chaos as an artisan"

- **Facebook** — older demographic (45-55)
  - Groups: "Artisans du bâtiment", "Auto-entrepreneurs France"
  - Local community groups

### 4. Paid Ads — Weight: 15%

**Google Ads:**
- Target: "logiciel CRM artisan", "gestion clients artisan"
- Budget: €500-1000/month (test)
- Need to own the search terms before competitors

**Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram):**
- Target: French males, 35-55, interested in entrepreneurship
- Lead form ads (simplified signup)
- Budget: €300-500/month (test)

**Avoid:**
- LinkedIn (wrong audience)
- Twitter/X (wrong demographic)

## Pricing & Trial Strategy

### Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Users | Key Features |
|------|-------|-------|--------------|
| **Solo** | €29/mo | 1 | Contacts, pipeline, reminders |
| **Pro** | €49/mo | 3 | Work logging, photos, daily summary |
| **Business** | €79/mo | Unlimited | Priority support, advanced reminders |

### Trial Strategy

**Free 14-day trial** — no credit card required
- Reduces friction
- Artisans hesitant to give credit card upfront
- Email capture for follow-up

**After trial:**
- If no signup → 1 follow-up email day 15
- Second email day 21
- Then monthly reminder (3 months max)

### Freemium Strategy (Consider Later)

**Free tier** — limited but functional:
- Up to 25 contacts
- 5 active deals
- No reminders

**Purpose:** Viral loop, get artisans using it, upgrade later

## Partner Strategy

### Key Partners

| Partner Type | Examples | Value |
|--------------|----------|-------|
| Building suppliers | Point.P, BigMat, VM, Castorama Pro | Distribution, trust |
| Trade guilds | Fédération du Bâtiment | Credibility, reach |
| Business services | Comptables, experts-comptables | Referrals |
| Tech partners | OVH, Scaleway | Co-marketing |

### Partnership Tactics

1. **Building supplier pilot**
   - Approach 1-2 local branches
   - Offer their clients 1 month free
   - They get to offer extra service

2. **Guild sponsorship**
   - Sponsor local artisan guild events
   - Speak at monthly meetings
   - Demo the product

3. **Accountant referrals**
   - Many accountants serve artisans
   - Offer them a dashboard to see clients
   - They refer during onboarding

## Key Go-to-Market Insights

1. **Beta users are your sales force** — give them a reason to talk about you
2. **Trade fairs > online ads** for this audience (physical trust)
3. **French language everywhere** — no English, ever
4. **Simple pricing, no confusion** — 3 tiers, clear value
5. **Partner with suppliers** — they already have the artisans' trust
6. **Persistence required** — artisans take 6+ months to decide
7. **Video > text** — they watch YouTube, don't read blogs
