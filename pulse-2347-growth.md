## The Case for SEO as Month 1 Primary

**SEO is the only digital channel Louis can execute alone, from scratch, with compounding returns.**

The settled assumption — "SEO is a Month 6+ long-tail play" — rests on a flawed premise: that French artisans don't search for software solutions. They do. Queries like "logiciel devis facture artisan gratuit," "application devis facture android," and "comment faire un devis professionnel telephone" represent active, transactional intent from the exact target persona. This is not exploratory content marketing — this is bottom-of-funnel, problem-solution SEO that converts.

**Why SEO beats prescriber outreach for Month 1-3:**

1. **Louis has zero prescribers.** He cannot call architects he doesn't know. He cannot ask property managers to recommend software they haven't seen work. Building prescriber relationships from cold takes months. SEO requires only content and a domain.

2. **SEO compounds; prescriber outreach doesn't.** A prescriber relationship serves one practitioner. A well-ranked article serves every artisan in France who searches the same query next month. The leverage is asymmetric in SEO's favor over a 3-month window.

3. **The "Month 6+ SEO" logic assumes a product worth googling.** If SEO starts at Month 6, it starts with zero indexed content, zero domain authority, and zero ranking position — just as the product is supposedly gaining traction. That's a 6-month head-start gap to climb. Better to build the foundation now, while Louis is already building the product.

4. **Technical implementation is trivial.** One Nuxt content page targeting "logiciel devis facture artisan" + basic structured data + a speed-optimized landing page. Louis writes the content himself (he knows the product), ships it in Sprint 0, and Google indexes it within weeks.

**What "SEO as Month 1 primary" means in practice:**
- Target 3-5 transactional keywords in the devis/facture/gestion artisan problem space
- One landing page optimized for conversion (U3 simplicity frame, U6 headline)
- Basic on-page SEO (meta titles, descriptions, heading structure)
- "Comment avez-vous connu l'app?" tracking live from Day 1
- No blog required for Month 1 — just intent-matched landing pages
- Community seeding (Month 4+) then amplifies what SEO started
