## Debate: Expert-Comptable Outreach Week 1 Is Premature

**Challenging D85:** Cold expert-comptable outreach in Week 1 wastes the highest-trust GTM asset on a cold audience.

---

### Core Argument

D85 resolves a conflict between D55 (expert-comptable = Phase 2) and the GTM strategy by moving expert-comptable outreach to Week 1, framing it as a "recommendation channel" distinct from the "data-sync portal" that remains Phase 2. The logic: get on the recommended software list early, before competitors do.

This is exactly backwards.

The expert-comptable is the single highest-trust referral channel available for a French artisan tool. An expert-comptable who recommends software to an artisan client is making a professional endorsement — one that affects that client's compliance, record-keeping, and relationship with their accountant for years. That recommendation only has weight if the expert-comptable has seen the product work in practice. Cold outreach in Week 1, before any beta artisan has used the product, before any mentions légales have been validated in production, before there is a single testimonial — this is not a recommendation request. It is a cold sales pitch to someone whose professional credibility is on the line.

---

### Four Specific Challenges to D85's Timing

**1. The expert-comptable's first question will have no answer.**
When you cold-email an expert-comptable in Week 1, the conversation will go: "Do you have clients using this?" "Can I see a sample facture with correct mentions légales for a BTP client?" "Which of your clients has validated the TVA calculation?" We have no beta users. We have no production data. We have a Sprint 0 build that hasn't been tested on a real device in the field. The expert-comptable will correctly conclude this is vaporware — and they will be right.

**2. A failed cold outreach burns the relationship before the product has a chance to prove itself.**
Expert-comptables are not a scalable cold acquisition channel — there are not thousands of them servicing small artisans in Caen. You have maybe 20-50 relevant expert-comptables in a reasonable radius. Each cold outreach that goes unanswered, or that results in a polite "not interested," closes a door. The window for re-engagement after a "not yet, come back when you have clients" is effectively forever. You get one shot. D85 blows it in Week 1.

**3. "Add to your recommended software list" is not a warm ask.**
The best-case outcome of Week 1 cold outreach is an expert-comptable saying "send me more info." That's not a win — that's an email in a folder they'll forget. To get on a recommended software list, an expert-comptable needs social proof: real users, real documents, real compliance validation. That takes 3-4 weeks minimum with an active beta.

**4. The Free tier means artisan adoption comes before accountant endorsement.**
D6 resolves that the Free tier IS the trial. Artisans will sign up, hit limits, and convert organically. By Week 4-6, there will be real beta users — artisans who have created real devis with real mentions légales. These are the users the expert-comptable wants to hear about. "I have 15 artisans using this in production" is a completely different conversation from "we're building something."

---

### Proposed Resolution

**Move expert-comptable outreach to Week 4-6.**

Prerequisites before outreach:
1. **10-20 active beta users** — artisans who have created and sent real devis in production
2. **1-2 written testimonials** — short, specific, from real artisans (not founders)
3. **Production-validated mentions légales** — at least one devis and one facture that have been sent to real clients and confirmed correct
4. **A sample document** the expert-comptable can review — not a mockup, a real document from a real beta user

The outreach itself changes: instead of "add us to your software list," lead with "we have artisans in your area using this for their devis and factures — would you like to see how it handles BTP client mentions?" This is consultative, not transactional. It respects the expert-comptable's need to validate before endorsing.

**GetApp/Capterra profiles claimed in Week 1** — this D85 item is correct and should be executed immediately. But claiming profiles is not outreach. It's infrastructure. Do it now.

---

### Verdict on D85

**D85 is partially correct, prematurely executed.**

The decision to separate recommendation outreach (Week 1) from data-sync portal (Phase 2) is sound. Expert-comptables as referral partners is the right long-term GTM play. But the execution — cold outreach to 5 expert-comptables in Week 1 before there is a single production user — gets the timing catastrophically wrong.

**D85 should be amended:** Expert-comptable outreach moves to Week 4-6, after active beta users exist. Week 1 action: claim GetApp/Capterra profiles only. Phase 2: data-sync portal. Week 4-6: warm outreach with testimonials, production documents, and specific beta user evidence.

The expert-comptable is the highest-trust referral channel. Treat it accordingly — don't waste it cold.
