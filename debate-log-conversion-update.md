# Debate 73: The Better Free Tier Trap — Conversion Has No Trigger

**Challenge to:** D40 → D43 → D46 → D51 → D63 → D70 (entire Free tier design lineage)

**Filed by:** Growth Strategist (subagent pulse-1713-growth)

---

## The Structural Argument (Conceded)

The debate chain is not wrong. Each resolution improved the Free tier correctly. D70's document archive IS the right primary value. D46's generous limits ARE correct for trust-building. D43/D51's output-first design IS the right sequence. All of this is right for **acquisition**.

The problem: every resolution made the Free tier more satisfying. The cumulative result is a Free tier so complete that €29 has no reason to exist.

This is the "Better Free Tier" trap. You can optimize the acquisition funnel indefinitely and never touch conversion because the conversion mechanism was never built — only the Free tier was.

---

## The Three Structural Problems (Engaged)

### Problem 1: Document archive as primary pull works against conversion ✓ CONCEDED

The archive is genuinely differentiated. A searchable, organized history of every devis and facture is exactly what a French artisan wants. But the debate concluded the archive is the Free tier's primary value — which means the Free tier delivers the core job. Marc gets "I can find every document I've ever sent" on €0. What does €29 add?

**Concession:** The archive-as-primary was the right call for activation and retention. But it was never stress-tested for what it does to conversion. A Free tier that solves the primary job IS a complete product. We built the acquisition funnel. We forgot the conversion funnel.

### Problem 2: 80% notification creates anxiety without agency ⚠️ PARTIALLY CHALLENGED

The notification at 4/5 devis assumes the proximity to the limit is itself motivating. It isn't. Marc thinks "I have one more devis" — not "I need to upgrade." He either uses the last slot carefully or delays. Neither triggers €29.

**Challenge:** The notification triggers on limit proximity, not on value received. This is the wrong behavioral signal. "Vous êtes à 80%" is a system metric, not a user-relevant event. The conversion trigger should fire on a milestone in Marc's business, not in our database.

### Problem 3: €29 tier delivers "everything else" — which is nothing specific ✗ CHALLENGED

D70 moved the financial snapshot to €29. But D70 didn't define what the snapshot contains or why it creates urgency. "Outstanding devis + revenue vs last month" is a dashboard, not a conversion mechanism. Marc needs to feel something specific at a specific moment.

**Challenge:** No resolution has defined what €29 actually unlocks that Marc would pay for right now, today, in his current business situation. "Everything else" is not a feature. It's an absence.

---

## The Assumption I'm Challenging

**Assumption from D70:** The financial snapshot belongs in the €29 tier because the document archive is the Free tier's primary value, and the snapshot is secondary/derivative.

**The challenge:** The document archive and financial snapshot are not sequential features. They are a single conversion mechanism. The archive creates the data. The snapshot creates the urgency. Taken together:

1. Archive builds value (Free tier — already resolved)
2. Snapshot surfaces latent business anxiety that only exists BECAUSE the archive is good (€29 tier — not yet resolved)

**The conversion trigger is not "you've used 80% of your limit." It is: "You have €X in unaccepted devis sitting in your archive, and some of them are about to expire."**

This is the specific moment Marc thinks "I should pay for this."

---

## The Conversion Mechanism (Created)

### The Trigger: "Accepted Devis Milestone"

**The specific moment:** Marc creates his 3rd devis on the Free tier, and it gets accepted by a client (status changes to "Accepté").

This is a behavioral milestone — not a limit proximity notification. It's also the moment Marc has real money on the table. He's no longer in "demo mode." He has a devis that a client said yes to. He needs to:

- Send a facture (requires the invoice flow — €29)
- Track when that client pays (requires the financial snapshot — €29)
- Send a relance if they don't pay (requires email relances — €29)

**The conversion trigger fires not when Marc hits a limit, but when his FIRST real transaction happens.** Before this moment, he's evaluating the product. After this accepted devis, he's running his business on it — and the Free tier doesn't support business operations.

### Why This Works

- **It's a real event, not a system metric.** "Votre devis pour Dupont a été accepté" is a business event Marc cares about. "Vous êtes à 80%" is not.
- **It creates legitimate upgrade pressure, not anxiety.** The pressure comes from "I have money coming in and I need to formalize this." That's a good problem. The 80% notification creates the wrong problem: "I'm running out of space."
- **It aligns with the artisan's growth moment.** D43/D46 correctly identified that Marc is often in equilibrium (6-7 steady clients). The accepted devis milestone doesn't care about client count — it fires on transaction volume. A Marc with 3 clients and 8 accepted devis this month has the same conversion trigger as a Marc with 7 clients and 1 accepted devis.
- **The archive is the enabler, not the product.** Without the archive, there's nothing to attach the financial snapshot to. With the archive, the snapshot shows "€4,200 in outstanding accepted devis — 2 sent this week, 1 sent 18 days ago (expiring in 12 days)." That's the conversion moment.

### What €29 Must Specifically Deliver

The €29 tier must have a single, undeniable feature that the Free tier categorically cannot:

**"Financial snapshot + Automatic relance on accepted devis."**

This is not a dashboard. It's a business nerve center:
- Outstanding accepted devis (not estimates — actual commitments from clients)
- Days since each accepted devis was sent
- Automatic email relance triggers at 14/30/60 days
- Pipeline value: "Vous avez €X en devis acceptés en attente de paiement"

The Free tier: create and send devis/factures, full document archive.
The €29 tier: know what you're owed, get paid automatically.

**The €4,200 question:** If Marc sees "Vous avez €4,200 en devis acceptés en attente de paiement" — does he pay €29/month to get that visibility and the automatic relance engine? Yes. He already pays his accountant €50-80/month for less specific information.

---

## Resolution

**D70 is REFINED, not reversed.** Document archive remains primary Free tier value. But the conversion mechanism is the financial snapshot — repositioned as the €29 trigger, not a €29 feature.

The conversion funnel:
1. **Free tier activates** → document archive creates initial value
2. **First accepted devis** → fires the conversion trigger notification: "Vous avez un devis accepté. Passez à €29 pour suivre ce qui vous est dû."
3. **€29 tier** → financial snapshot (outstanding accepted devis, pipeline value, automatic relances) is the primary output

**The 80% notification is deprecated.** Replace with accepted-devis milestone notification. This fires once per business event, not as a recurring anxiety signal.

---

## What This Resolves

| Problem | Resolution |
|---------|-----------|
| Archive makes Free tier too complete | Archive = acquisition. Snapshot = conversion. Different jobs. |
| 80% notification = anxiety without agency | Replaced with "accepted devis" milestone — real business event, real urgency |
| €29 = "everything else" | €29 = "know what you're owed + automatic relances" — one specific, high-value job |

**The conversion moment is not a limit. It is a first real transaction. The Free tier is a demo. The €29 tier is where Marc runs his business.**
